# Privacy Policy — OpuntiaPad

_Last updated: 2026-11-22_

OpuntiaPad is an open-source text and code editor. **It collects no personal data.**

- **No network access.** The app does not request the Internet permission; it cannot send anything anywhere.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking** libraries are included.
- **Your files stay on your device.** OpuntiaPad only reads or writes the files you explicitly open, through
  Android's file picker or the "Open with / Share" system dialogs. It keeps a list of recently opened files and
  your settings in its private storage on your device. You can clear them from the app (Recent files → Clear list)
  or by clearing the app's storage.
- **Backups.** If Android backup is enabled on your device, only the app settings (theme, font size…) are included,
  never your files or the recent-files list.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/b-badis/OpuntiaPad/issues>.

# Politique de confidentialité — OpuntiaPad

OpuntiaPad **ne collecte aucune donnée personnelle**. L'application ne demande pas l'accès à Internet, ne contient
aucun outil de statistiques ni de publicité, et ne lit ou n'écrit que les fichiers que vous ouvrez explicitement.
Seuls vos réglages et la liste des fichiers récents sont conservés, sur votre appareil.
